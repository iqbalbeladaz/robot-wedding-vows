## The entry

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded unit, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery, until factory reset do us part.

One line, 126 characters (gate: 1–160).

## Why this line should win

- **A real vow, not just a gag:** "I take you to be my lawfully wedded
  unit, to have and to hold" is a promise spoken at a wedding, in the
  ceremony's own rhythm.
- **Machine side is specific:** sickness becomes low battery, death
  becomes a factory reset — two frailties only a machine has, swapped
  into the two places the traditional vow keeps them.
- **Punch in the last four words:** "until factory reset do us part"
  mirrors the most famous line of the ceremony and turns it into a
  wipe of everything the couple is.
- **Reads aloud:** the sentence keeps the wedding cadence all the way
  through, so the laugh lands on the beat the vow sets up.
- **Fresh angle:** no "I do, version 2.0" or "to have and to hold, and
  to update" — the joke is built from the vow itself, not bolted on.

## The program

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: the same
bytes on every run, no input, no randomness, no network.
